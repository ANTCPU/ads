# ANTCPU ADS Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the ANTCPU ADS community a safe, open, and harassment-free experience for everyone—regardless of background, experience level, identity, or perspective. 

We are committed to building an environment that fosters collaboration, innovation, and respect.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:**
* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward fellow developers and contributors.
* Respecting differing viewpoints, architectural approaches, and experiences.
* Accepting constructive feedback gracefully and offering it with empathy.
* Owning our mistakes, apologizing when we impact others, and learning from the experience.
* Prioritizing the long-term health and growth of the community above individual interests.

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
* The use of sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual advances.
* Trolling, personal attacks, insults, or hostile political/personal arguments.
* Public or private harassment of any kind.
* Publishing private information (such as personal addresses or emails) without explicit consent.
* Any other conduct that is reasonably considered unprofessional or inappropriate in a community setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both within all repository spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests) and in public communication channels associated with the ANTCPU ADS ecosystem.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers directly. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion to maintain a healthy ecosystem for everyone building together 🚀.
